djvj

Automated, audio-reactive visuals for a solo DJ set.

Open output
for the big screen Open control
stays on your laptop
  1. Open the output window, drag it to the big screen, click once to allow audio input, press F.
  2. Open control on the laptop screen. Pick the audio input there: built-in mic, a USB interface, or BlackHole.
  3. No audio yet? Press M for a simulated demo signal.

Use Chrome. Everything runs locally and offline.
